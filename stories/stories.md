# Stories and Reflections


When I first learned about Linus Torvalds, I was amazed by how one person could create something that changed the way millions of people work. His insistence on meritocracy and action over endless discussion really shaped how I approach my own coding projects. Reading about his work made me realize that great software isn’t just about clever code—it’s about creating a culture where people can collaborate and improve together.  
*— Kaleab, Linux user: kal_x*

---
I still remember setting up my first Linux server to host a personal project. I chose Linux because I knew it was reliable, scalable, and open. That experience opened my eyes to the power of Linux-based products. Things like Docker, Git, and even Android all feel connected to the same mindset Linus created. Without Linux, I probably wouldn’t be as confident deploying and managing servers today.  
*— Kaleab, Linux user: kal_x*
