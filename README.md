# open-letter-to-linus

**open-letter-to-linus** is a new, open space for developers, Linux users, and fans to come together and share their thoughts about **Linus Torvalds**, Linux, and the technologies built because of them.

Here, you can:
- Explore Linus’ quotes and reflect on their meaning ([quotes.md](quotes/quotes.md))
- Read stories about how Linux and Linux-based projects impacted people’s lives ([stories.md](stories/stories.md))
- Share your own experiences, reflections, or favorite technologies ([entries](entries))

This is not just a repository — it’s a **spot to meet and connect with other Linux users and fans**. Everyone can read, everyone can write, and contributions are welcome from anyone who wants to leave their mark.
