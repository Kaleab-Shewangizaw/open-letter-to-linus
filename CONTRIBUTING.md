# Contributing to open-letter-to-linus

Thank you for wanting to contribute! This repository is a shared space where developers come together to write about **Linus Torvalds**, Linux, and the technologies and culture he inspired.

Contributions are welcome from everyone, whether you are a beginner, experienced developer, or simply someone who wants to share a story or quote.

---

## How to Contribute

### 1. Fork the repository
Click the "Fork" button on GitHub to create your own copy of this repository.

### 2. Make your contribution
This repository has three main sections:

- `/entries`  
  Share technologies, projects, or ideas that exist because of Linux or Linus’ work. One file per contribution is recommended.

- `/quotes/quotes.md`  
  Add a Linus Torvalds quote with a short explanation if you like. Include your name or GitHub handle after the explanation.

- `/stories/stories.md`  
  Add a personal story, reflection, or experience about Linus, Linux, or products built on Linux. Include your name or GitHub handle at the end.

> Each contribution should be added above the final instruction line in each file.  

---

### 3. Open a Pull Request
Once your changes are ready, open a pull request. Describe briefly what you added. A reviewer may give feedback, but your contribution will likely be accepted quickly.

---

## Contribution Guidelines

- Be respectful and constructive
- Focus on Linus, Linux, or related technologies
- Keep entries clear and readable
- One file per entry for `/entries`
- Quotes and stories should include your name or GitHub handle
- If you’re unsure where your contribution fits, just pick the most appropriate section

---

## Why Contribute

This is your chance to leave a small mark on something collective — a living, open letter to Linus Torvalds. Your contribution might inspire others or help someone see the impact of Linux in a new way.

---

Thank you for being part of this. Open like Linux, this is a space for everyone.
